Privacy Policy

Khmer Names · Effective 25 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Khmer Names mobile app for Android and iOS (the "App"), made by App Peppers ("we", "us"), handles information.

1. Summary

  • The App has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or other contact details.
  • Your settings and bookmarked names stay on your device. We do not send them to our servers.
  • The App shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data to serve and measure ads.

2. Information stored on your device

The App saves your language and your bookmarked names on your device so they are there the next time you open it. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the App. The list of names, their meanings and the naming guides are built into the App, so your searches and filters are not sent to us.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob may collect and use information such as:

  • device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID or Apple's IDFA (on iOS, only if you allow tracking);
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device type, operating system and app version;
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data such as crash logs.

Google uses this information to show ads, which may be personalized, to measure ad performance and to prevent fraud. You can learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Your choices

  • Ad personalization: you can manage how Google personalizes ads at My Ad Center.
  • iOS: the App asks for permission before tracking. You can change it any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads).
  • You can stop all data collection by the App by uninstalling it.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising data collected by Google as described above, we do not share information with third parties, because we do not collect it in the first place. When you use "Share this app" or "Rate this app", your device's share sheet or app store opens, and what you do there is covered by those services.

6. Security

Data sent by the AdMob SDK is encrypted in transit. Data stored by the App stays in your device's app storage.

7. Children

The App is meant for parents and other adults choosing a name. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. Because we do not hold personal information about you, most requests are best directed to Google for ad data. You can still contact us with any question.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page.
